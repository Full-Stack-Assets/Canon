## Portfolio operating rule

The next dollar should come from **selling an existing outcome**, not creating another product. New feature/build work should be subordinate to one of these gates:

1. verified buyer demand;
2. paid pilot or transaction;
3. measurable conversion/retention;
4. required delivery blocker for an already-qualified opportunity;
5. reusable infrastructure serving multiple top-tier revenue workstreams.
