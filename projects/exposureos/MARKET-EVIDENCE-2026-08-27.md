# ExposureOS market evidence — 2026-08-27

Status: source-backed market evidence; not customer-payment proof.

## Customer acquisition pain
FreshBooks, `2025 State of U.S. Small Business` (updated 2026-01-30): survey of 750 U.S. small business owners; 47% said finding customers is harder than ever and identified customer acquisition as the top growth challenge for the second consecutive year.
Source: https://www.freshbooks.com/hub/feature-news/state-us-small-business-report

## Home-services lead economics
LocaliQ, `2025 Search Ad Benchmarks for Home Services`: analysis of 3,200+ search ad campaigns from April 2024 through March 2025. Overall home-services average cost per lead was $90.92. Reported category CPLs include air conditioning installation/repair $127.74, heating/furnaces $129.02, plumbing $129.02, electricians/electrical contractors $93.69, and roofing/gutters $228.15. The report states CPL increased for 69% of home-services businesses, averaging +10.51% year over year.
Source: https://localiq.com/blog/home-services-search-advertising-benchmarks/

## Reputation and multi-surface discovery
BrightLocal, `Local Consumer Review Survey 2026` (2026-02-11): 97% of surveyed consumers read local-business reviews; consumers increasingly expect recent/high ratings; ChatGPT and other AI tools rose to third place among local recommendation sources.
Source: https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/

BrightLocal, `Nearly Half of Consumers are Asking AI for Business Recommendations` (2026-03-10): 45% of consumers reported using AI tools for local-business recommendations in the prior year, up from 6%; 31% reported ChatGPT use for business recommendations.
Source: https://www.brightlocal.com/research/lcrs-ai-trust/

BrightLocal, `Where are your customers really searching?` (2026-07): 23% used AI during their most recent local-business search, 31% use AI tools for local recommendations at least monthly, and only 18% of AI-using consumers were ready to contact the recommended business without further verification.
Source: https://www.brightlocal.com/research/consumer-search-behavior-channels/

BrightLocal, `6% of consumers just click the top result. So what wins over the other 94%?` (2026-07-15): consumer choice is influenced by reviews, location, pricing clarity, listing completeness, and freshness, supporting ExposureOS's discovery-plus-trust wedge rather than a rank-only product.
Source: https://www.brightlocal.com/research/consumer-search-behavior-decisions/

## Evidence interpretation
These sources support the existence and urgency of the problem cohort and the economic importance of lead recovery and reputation/discovery quality. They do not prove willingness to pay for ExposureOS, conversion rate, churn, or realized ROI. Those remain blocked until first-party customer evidence exists.
