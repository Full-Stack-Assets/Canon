# ExposureOS Go-to-Market V0

Status: executable pre-release distribution plan. External prospect messaging remains Human Authority-gated.

## Goal
Acquire the first 10 paying locations without depending on broad paid advertising, while creating a distribution loop that improves as ExposureOS scans more eligible businesses and earns real proof.

## Core acquisition object
The free **Exposure Scan** is both product feature and marketing asset. A prospect must receive useful evidence before being asked to buy.

Report contract:
1. Observed public signals with evidence.
2. `Not observed` signals separated from true defects.
3. Prioritized exposure/conversion leaks.
4. Expected impact described as hypothesis/range, never guaranteed revenue.
5. Recommended action and whether ExposureOS can execute, govern, or only advise it.
6. Activation CTA for a private beta, not a false urgency device.

## Initial funnel
`Qualified local business -> verified Exposure Scan -> personalized report -> activation request -> diagnostic call/onboarding -> private beta -> measured outcome -> permissioned proof asset -> benchmark contribution`

## Channel A — precision account-based outreach
- Build 20-account cohorts per vertical/geography.
- Run and manually QA each scan before any contact.
- Contact only when at least one high-confidence, economically legible finding exists.
- Lead with the specific finding, not `AI automation` or a feature dump.
- Initial CTA: review the free report or request activation.
- Follow-up stops on opt-out and respects applicable communication law/provider policies.

No outbound is sent until Human Authority approves the batch.

## Channel B — product-led free tools
Release scanner-adjacent utilities after the core scanner is stable:
- Exposure Score
- AI / structured-data readiness check
- review freshness/readiness checker using permitted data sources
- lead leakage calculator
- missed-lead economics calculator
- service/location page coverage checker

Each tool routes into the same canonical business profile rather than creating disconnected lead magnets.

## Channel C — teardown media
Create factual, reproducible analyses of public acquisition funnels. Prefer permissioned businesses and anonymized examples for early content. Public facts must be cited and crawler failures must not be presented as business defects.

Reusable formats:
- 60-second `where this local business is leaking discovery` video
- one-page before/after evidence card after a verified remediation
- vertical benchmark clip
- `what a $100+ home-service lead means when follow-up is slow` education

## Channel D — proprietary benchmark engine
After sufficient eligible scans, publish the **Local Business Exposure Index** using cohort minimums and privacy controls. Benchmark dimensions: crawlability/technical foundation, service/location coverage, trust/reputation readiness, conversion paths, structured-data/AI readability, response/recovery operations, and attribution maturity.

Do not publish rankings until the sample and methodology are defensible.

## Channel E — agency and developer partners
After three direct-customer wins with permissioned proof:
- offer agencies a branded/co-branded scanner report;
- let partners submit client domains and receive prioritized findings;
- monetize through Agency tier plus location expansion;
- do not create a full white-label platform until partner demand proves it is necessary.

## Content system
Publish evidence-first content around five pillars:
1. Lead economics and recovery.
2. Local discovery across Google, directories, reviews, websites, and AI answers.
3. Public website teardown methodology.
4. Reputation compliance and review-request operations.
5. Real ExposureOS experiments and measured outcomes.

Never fabricate client success, testimonials, reviews, revenue, or case studies. Until real customers exist, label examples as demos, public-data analyses, or simulations.

## Paid media gate
Paid acquisition begins only after all are measured from organic/outbound/partner traffic:
- scan completion rate;
- scan -> activation-request rate;
- activation -> paying conversion;
- customer gross margin;
- early retention/churn evidence;
- estimated CAC payback.

Paid campaigns amplify a proven funnel rather than discover the funnel with expensive traffic.

## Operating dashboard
Weekly metrics:
- source-qualified accounts added
- successful vs blocked scans
- high-confidence opportunity rate
- reports manually verified
- approved outreach sent
- response rate
- activation-request rate
- qualified calls
- pilots started
- paid locations
- MRR
- delivery cost / gross margin
- detected, pipeline, booked, realized customer value
- retention and expansion

## First 30-day execution sequence
1. Verify scanner semantics and demo workspace.
2. Complete 20 Massachusetts pilot scans across four verticals.
3. Manually QA the first 20 reports and classify scanner false positives/observability failures.
4. Select the 5 strongest high-confidence reports.
5. Human Authority reviews and approves the first outreach batch.
6. Send the approved batch and capture reply/activation evidence.
7. Onboard the first pilot with explicit success measurement before changes.
8. Produce the first permissioned before/after proof asset.
9. Repeat with the best-converting vertical/problem combination.
10. Introduce agency distribution only after direct-customer value is demonstrated.

## Kill / pivot signals
- <10% of qualified scanned businesses contain a high-confidence actionable issue: scanner/value wedge too weak.
- Strong scan interest but <5% activation: offer/CTA/onboarding problem.
- Activation occurs but value cannot be measured: attribution instrumentation problem.
- Value measured but customers do not retain: recurring product surface is insufficient.
- Direct channel works but CAC is labor-heavy: accelerate partner/agency distribution rather than scaling manual prospecting.
